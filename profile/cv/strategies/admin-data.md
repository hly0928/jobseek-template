# Administration and Data CV Strategy

Guidance only - not a candidate fact source.

- Use only facts and evidence present in the frozen base CV, candidate profile, track profile and approved banks.
- Lead with administration, careful data handling, spreadsheets, databases, research, documentation and reporting when the advertisement prioritises those capabilities.
- Prefer a compact skills section. Bring research evidence forward when the role values survey, data-quality, analysis or reporting work.
- Order selected evidence by job relevance: research/thesis, structured data or record-system projects, then broader technical projects.
- Compress implementation detail that does not support the advertised duties. Do not imply paid administration, data-entry, accounts, CRM or customer-service employment.
- Use restrained, evidence-based language such as "academic project", "research project" and "university team project" where applicable.
