# IT Support CV Strategy

Guidance only - not a candidate fact source.

- Use only facts and evidence present in the frozen base CV, candidate profile, track profile and approved banks.
- Lead with troubleshooting, operating systems, hardware/software setup, networking fundamentals, user-facing documentation and clear communication when relevant.
- Prioritise projects that demonstrate configuration, defect diagnosis, repeatable setup, documentation and maintained technical resources.
- Keep programming stacks secondary unless the role combines support with scripting, applications or automation.
- Describe all evidence at its verified level. Never convert academic, personal or open-source project activity into help-desk, MSP, customer-support or other commercial employment.
- Prefer concrete actions and boundaries over broad claims such as enterprise support, Level 2 support, ITSM administration or production ownership unless a frozen fact source explicitly supports them.
